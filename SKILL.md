# Sunge Narrative Writing

Build a readable Chinese narrative with a clear truth contract and an original expression system. Extract mechanisms from references; never reproduce their distinctive wording, plot, character relations, object combinations, or authorial persona.

This is an independent, unofficial writing method. It is not a “winner narrative” or personality school, and it is not affiliated with or endorsed by any person or similarly themed project.

## Start With Two Decisions

1. Label the mode: `fiction`, `narrative nonfiction`, `brand/case story`, or `hybrid`.
2. Write the one-sentence reading contract: what tension the reader is promised, and what the text is allowed to claim.

For nonfiction, a public post, interview, allegation, memory, or first-person account proves only that the statement was made. It does not prove the underlying event. Read [references/fact-boundaries.md](references/fact-boundaries.md) before drafting nonfiction, real-person material, or sensitive claims.

## Ten Mechanisms

1. **Conflict formula:** define `visible capability or order` against `one small need that remains unmet`.
2. **Double-scale hook:** collide two concrete scales, costs, time spans, or consequences without copying a source's unusual comparison.
3. **Hidden question:** choose the single question the reader is waiting to see answered. Treat it as a working hypothesis, not a fact about another person's motive or causality; validate it with evidence or keep it visibly open.
4. **Beat path:** for long relationship, obsession, or rise-and-fall narratives, consider `crisis cold open → distant cause → encounter → evidence of closeness → anomaly accumulation → one rule breaks → aftermath returns`. Adapt or replace this path when the material calls for another structure.
5. **Motif register:** track each motif through `appearance → variation → changed return`. Use no more than four motifs; short work usually needs one dominant and one supporting motif.
6. **Repeat–exception engine:** establish a behavior or response as a rule, vary its circumstances, then let one consequential exception alter the meaning of earlier repetitions.
7. **Scene evidence:** give every major scene an observable carrier—action, labor, object, sound, spatial constraint, transaction, or altered routine.
8. **Subtextual dialogue:** let speakers request, evade, bargain, test, or withdraw. Remove lines whose only job is explanation.
9. **Limited viewpoint:** reveal only what the viewpoint can observe or reasonably infer. Track changes in address terms and naming only when they signal real relational distance.
10. **Consequence objects and return:** after the turn, inventory what remains—unfinished work, residue, reminders, unused objects, or routines still running—and choose one transformed item for the ending.

For the detailed mechanics and failure modes, read [references/narrative-mechanics.md](references/narrative-mechanics.md). When starting from loose material, copy [assets/narrative-blueprint.md](assets/narrative-blueprint.md) and fill only the sections the task needs.

## Drafting Invariants

- Preserve the user's subject, genre, audience, length, and publication goal.
- Create a new scene order and sentence architecture. Do not paraphrase a source passage line by line.
- If the comparison source is unavailable, claim only a mechanism-level transformation and state that source similarity has not been verified.
- Build one causal scene spine. Each staged scene must change knowledge, power, relationship, cost, or intention.
- Cut in time after pressure; enter the next scene at its first consequence.
- Compress travel and background; expand decisions, silences, manual actions, and aftermath.
- At emotional peaks, prefer body, routine, inventory, or failed action over named emotion and commentary.
- Keep a setup-payoff ledger; delete seeds that do not pay off and returns that were not prepared.
- Do not invent quotations, measurements, dates, diagnoses, motives, transactions, or private conduct in nonfiction.
- When verified material cannot support the requested length, deliver a shorter honest draft or request more evidence; do not manufacture detail as padding.
- If a requested claim cannot be verified, attribute it, qualify it, omit it, or convert the piece to clearly labeled fiction with the user's agreement.
- A fiction disclaimer does not license recognizable allegations about real people. Remove identifying details or obtain appropriate review for publication-sensitive work.

If a user asks for close imitation of a living writer, respond in this order: decline reproduction of the personal voice, name the transferable mechanisms you can use, then offer an original plan and confirm whether the truth mode is fiction or nonfiction when that choice is not already clear.

## Final Pass

Read [references/revision-scorecard.md](references/revision-scorecard.md) for publication-ready work. Revise in this order:

1. truth, privacy, and consent risks;
2. source-distance and originality;
3. causal, repetition, motif, and setup-payoff integrity;
4. scene pressure, viewpoint, and pacing;
5. sentence-level polish.

Return the requested artifact, plus a brief fact-boundary note when real people, current events, or disputed claims are involved.
