# Revision Scorecard

Use this for a publication-ready narrative. Fix hard failures before scoring.

## Hard Failures

- mode or truth contract is unclear;
- an unverified private claim is stated as fact;
- invented quotation or precision appears in nonfiction;
- distinctive source phrasing, plot, object combination, or character mapping remains recognizable;
- the user asked for direct imitation of a living writer and the output preserves that personal voice;
- the opening promises a conflict the ending does not address.
- a source-distance pass is claimed even though the comparison source was unavailable.

## 120-Point Gate

| Dimension | Points | Full-credit test |
|---|---:|---|
| truth and publication ethics | 15 | sensitive claims have statuses and evidence-matched wording |
| originality boundary | 15 | the piece has its own scene order, objects, characters, and sentence design |
| central conflict | 10 | capability and unmet need create sustained pressure |
| opening and hidden question | 10 | concrete tension appears immediately and one question governs the read |
| scene causality and evidence | 10 | scenes change conditions; details perform visible work |
| motifs | 10 | no more than four; each retained motif varies and returns with changed meaning |
| setup and payoff | 10 | meaningful seeds return consequentially; false seeds are removed |
| repetition and exception | 10 | a real rule is established, varied, and broken once with consequence |
| number integrity | 5 | numbers are sourced, calculated, clearly fictional, or removed |
| dialogue and subtext | 5 | lines act on leverage instead of explaining theme |
| emotion through evidence | 5 | aftermath carries the peak without emotional overstatement |
| limited viewpoint and naming | 5 | observation, inference, and address changes remain controlled |
| rhythm and time distance | 5 | compression and expansion track narrative pressure |
| ending remainder | 5 | the action closes through transformed evidence before the moral is stated |

Recommended publication threshold: 96/120 with no hard failures.

## Source-Distance Audit

Compare against each major influence:

- Is the opening built from the same unusual comparison?
- Does the new piece use the same sequence of reveals?
- Does an object perform the same scene-by-scene role?
- Do character roles map one-to-one?
- Are distinctive sentence patterns, punch lines, or metaphors preserved?

If two or more answers are yes, redesign at the scene level. Synonym replacement is not transformation.

## Read-Aloud Pass

Read the first 150 characters, the central turn, and the final 200 characters aloud. Mark where:

- context arrives before curiosity;
- explanation repeats what an image already showed;
- every paragraph has the same length;
- dialogue sounds like an interview transcript or essay paragraph;
- the final sentence tells the reader what to feel.

Cut explanation before adding polish.
